## About the project
Technical is a complete overhaul of the standard Mindustry experience. My primary goal was to add content that differs significantly from the base game, providing a fresh experience while still retaining the core _Mindustry_ feel.

While the mod focuses on quality over quantity, every block introduces unique mechanics, so every one gives new, **fair** challenge. Technical is a complete overhaul of the standard Mindustry experience.

### I love Mindustry, but it's not perfect (this isn't hate speech, just a list of minor points):
- On Serpulo it feels too fast and then you have a lot of new sectors to overcome, but no new mechanics and after some time this feels _repeatable_
- On Erekir every sector has a lot to give you with new mechanics, but it's really short and gives a lot of new blocks at the end (Neoplasia Reactor) that you can't even test
- In general when game thinks it's smart and makes you feel stupid when that one dumb Avert slowly and gradually evaporates your entire base on the other side of the map, so you won't notice

### Experience I absolutely love (experience I wanted to recreate in this mod):
- the moment you need to use some unit to go over the wall destroy defences from the back and give space for more powerful to come
- when the game feels overcomplicated, you have a lot of tangled wires and belts, and the only person that can repair an issue is you
- learning: I love to learn new mechanics that get harder and harder over time (so the game gives a challenge)

### I think that flying units are annoying as hell (on Erekir especially), so they are not planned in my mod (except for transportation / ammunition). Why?
- No need for tactical ordering of units deeper and deeper inside enemy base just fly over it and destroy the core.
- If you want to make anti-air base you need to spam turrets all over your base (or read code of Mindustry and know that for some reason, that one annoying Antumbra likes batteries and trap it there)
- No ground traps like trap hooks, darts, nothing will affect them (balancing reasons)
