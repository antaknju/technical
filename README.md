## Content
This adds train transportation (it needs a rework), inserters, efficiency crafters, rain collectors, new biomes, mechanical power, heat transportation with liquid, cooling system

## About the project
Technical is a complete overhaul of the standard Mindustry game play. My primary goal was to add content that differs significantly from the base game, providing a fresh experience while still remaining _Mindustry_.

Another thing worth mentioning is that: I AM NOT AN ARTIST, I am just a hobbyist programmer that drew all the sprites, so they look questionable... but when everything is ugly... you can't really tell a difference?
Most of the mechanics and methods of crafting inside this mod are based on realistic industrial processes.

### Experience I absolutely love (experience I wanted to recreate in this mod):
- the moment you need to use small unit, to go over the wall, to destroy defenses from the back and open the entrance for more powerful ones
- when the game feels overcomplicated, you have a lot of tangled wires and belts, and the only person that actually understands what is happening is you

### I think that flying units are really annoying (especially on Erekir), so they are not planned in this mod (except for transportation). Why?
- No need for tactical units placement, and strategic order of attacks, you can just fly over enemy base and destroy the core.
- If you want to make anti-air base you need to spam turrets all over your base (or read code of Mindustry, which is very intresting and absolutely worth reading, and know how the AI works)
- No ground traps like trap hooks, darts or boulders would affect them
